2025/26 Panini Origins Basketball Hobby 12-Box Case (Preorder)

Product Details
UPC: 124
Year: 2025/26

Preorder ! Your case will be shipped around September the 3rd

Each Box contains 2 Autographs, 1 Memorabilia Card, 3 Base Parallels, and 2 Inserts/Insert Parallels, on average!

Origins is back for the 2025-26 season! Look for a stunning display of Autograph, Memorabilia and Insert/Parallel content!

Search for Autographs of hot rookies from the 2025 Draft and a blend of current, retired, and NIL players in Rookie Jersey Autographs, Rookie Autographs, Elevation Signatures, Blood Moon Signatures, Provenance Autographs, and Constellations Signatures!

Returning this year, find the popular SSP's such as White Tiger Eyes, Black Snake Eyes, Big Bang, and the All-New Panther Eyes! Also search for the popular Base Gala, new to Origins this year! Collect new inserts such as Originals and the all-rookie set On the Horizon!

Find an array of parallel content in the Base set, which includes Veterans, Rookies, Legends, and NIL stars from across the sport! Look for the following parallels: Red (#'d/199), Maroon (#'d/135), Blue (#'d/99), Orange (#'d/75), White (#'d/60), Neon Stripes (#'d/49), Pink (#'d/35), Turqoise (#'d/25), Tessellated, Opal (#'d/15), Gold (#'d/10), Green (#'d/5), and the One-of-One Black Parallel!

Chase the popular Booklet cards in Six Star Signatures and Rookie Auto Booklet Patch! Also look for the new Rookie Stars Dual Patch Signatures Booklet and Origins of Greatness Booklet Auto! Look for One-of-One Black parallels in each set!

Search for new Autograph sets including OrigiNation Autographs, Rookie Acetate Autographs, Legendary Patch Autos, Triple Signatures, Dead of Night Signatures, Blood Moon Signatures, Eclipse Signatures, and Lights of the Night Signatures!

Look for all-new Memorabilia sets such as Dual Swatches and Rookie Hats, which will feature Draft Night-worn hats of some of the top rookies from the 2025 Draft!

Configuration: 10 Cards Per Box, 12 Box Per Case
